At a glance

HouseClear provides full and partial house clearances throughout North Wales, including Mold, Buckley, Flint, Holywell, with quotes usually provided the same day.

  • Local team covering the whole of North Wales
  • Registered waste carrier — digital waste transfer note with every clearance
  • Fully insured; probate, bereavement, landlord and hoarder clearances handled
  • Reusable furniture donated to local charities where possible
  • Fixed quotes from photos or a free, no-obligation visit

HouseClear provides house clearances across North Wales — Flintshire, Denbighshire and Wrexham — from our base in Deeside. That puts us minutes from Mold, Buckley and Connah's Quay, half an hour from Wrexham, and an easy run along the A55 to Rhyl, Prestatyn and the Vale of Clwyd.

Being based on the Welsh side of the border matters for more than travel time. Waste carried in Wales must go under a registration valid for Wales, and ours is — every North Wales house clearance we do is documented with a digital waste transfer note, and reusable furniture is donated through North Wales charities so it stays useful locally rather than travelling back over the border to landfill.

The housing across these three counties could hardly be more varied, and so is our work: Deeside terraces and Flint's estate housing, Mold's market-town homes, Wrexham's former mining-village terraces and newer estates, retirement bungalows and seafront flats along the coast at Rhyl and Prestatyn, and farmhouses with outbuildings through the Vale of Clwyd and the hills above Ruthin and Llangollen. Every property is priced individually, from photos or a free visit.

Much of our North Wales work is probate and bereavement clearances — often for families who live elsewhere and need someone local they can trust with keys, keepsakes and a completion date. We collect keys from solicitors and agents, set aside documents and valuables, share photos as we go, and leave the property swept and locked. Landlord turnarounds, downsizing moves and full clearances before a sale make up the rest.

House clearance in North Wales — common questions

Which parts of North Wales do you cover?

Flintshire, Denbighshire and Wrexham in full — Deeside, Mold, Buckley, Flint, Holywell, Wrexham and its villages, and the coast and Vale of Clwyd from Rhyl and Prestatyn to Denbigh, Ruthin and Llangollen. If you are further west along the coast, ask — we can often help.

Is your waste carrier licence valid in Wales?

Yes. Our Environment Agency registration (CBDU464597) is valid for waste carried in both England and Wales, and every North Wales clearance is documented with a digital waste transfer note. You can verify the registration on the public register in under a minute.

Can you clear a house in North Wales if I live elsewhere?

Yes — this is a large part of our North Wales work. We collect keys from the solicitor or estate agent, send photos before and after, box keepsakes for collection or delivery, and leave the property swept and secure. Invoices can be addressed to the estate.

Do you cover rural properties and farms?

Yes. Long drives, outbuildings, barns full of decades of accumulation and properties with difficult access are all familiar work through the Vale of Clwyd and the hills around Ruthin and Llangollen. Just mention access when you enquire so we bring the right vehicle.

How much do house clearances in North Wales cost?

House clearances across North Wales are priced by volume — how much the contents fill in the van — adjusted for access and any items needing special handling. Send photos for a fixed same-day quote; where furniture or contents have resale value, we offset that against the price. The quote you agree is the price you pay.

Will you clear the property carefully and respectfully?

Yes. Our uniformed, insured team protects floors, doorways and staircases as they work, and we set aside documents, photographs and valuables we come across for you to keep. Whether it is a probate, bereavement or general clearance anywhere in North Wales, we work at your pace and treat every home with care.
