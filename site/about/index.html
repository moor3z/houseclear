About HouseClear

Who we are, how we work, and the credentials behind every clearance.

HouseClear is a professional house clearance company serving Cheshire, Flintshire, Denbighshire, Liverpool, Wrexham and the Wirral. HouseClear is a trading name of Tidy Up Ltd, an established Chester-based clearance and waste management company — so behind the HouseClear name sits an experienced team, existing insurance, and a current Environment Agency waste carrier registration held by Tidy Up Ltd. We clear houses, flats, garages, lofts and commercial-adjacent domestic properties for homeowners, landlords, estate agents, solicitors and families dealing with probate or bereavement.

Our credentials

  • Registered waste carrier — Environment Agency registration CBDU464597 (held by Tidy Up Ltd), valid for our work in both England and Wales, and verifiable on the public register.
  • Fully insured — public liability insurance covers every clearance we carry out, and certificates are supplied to agents, solicitors and housing providers on request.
  • Digital waste transfer notes — every clearance is documented with a digital waste transfer note, giving you legal proof of correct disposal.
  • VAT registered — VAT invoices are provided on completion (VAT No. 441151045), and can be addressed to an estate for probate accounting.

Part of the Tidy Up Ltd family

HouseClear is one of several trading names of Tidy Up Ltd, a Deeside-based clearance and waste management company. For general waste removal and rubbish collection rather than a property clearance, our sister brand ClearWaste handles that side of the business — same team, same licence, same paperwork. Whichever brand you contact, you are dealing with the same established local company.

How we handle what we clear

Every load is sorted before disposal. Reusable furniture and household goods are donated to charities local to the clearance or resold; metals, timber, cardboard and other recyclables are recovered at licensed transfer stations; only genuine waste goes for disposal. Where contents have resale value, that value is offset against your quote.

Who we work with

Alongside private households, HouseClear works directly with solicitors and probate professionals, letting agents and landlords, estate agents preparing properties for sale, and housing providers turning around void properties. We are used to being someone's contractor of record — supplying compliance documents up front, working to completion dates, and invoicing on account where needed.

Where we work

Our teams cover Chester, Ellesmere Port and Northwich across Cheshire; Mold, Buckley and Deeside in Flintshire; Rhyl, Prestatyn, Denbigh and Ruthin in Denbighshire; Wrexham and its villages; the whole of Liverpool; and the Wirral peninsula from Birkenhead to West Kirby. See our areas page for local details.
