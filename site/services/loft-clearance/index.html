At a glance

HouseClear provides loft clearance across Cheshire, Flintshire, Denbighshire, Liverpool, Wrexham and the Wirral, with fixed quotes usually provided the same day.

  • Registered waste carrier — digital waste transfer note with every job
  • Fully insured, uniformed local team
  • Fixed quotes from photos, a description or a free visit
  • Reusable items donated, resold or recycled before anything is disposed of
  • VAT invoices provided; finance available on larger clearances

Clearing a loft yourself means hours on a ladder passing boxes down one at a time. Our team does it safely and quickly — we bring our own ladders and lighting, board-walk carefully across joists where needed, and carry everything down and out to the van.

Loft clearances are most often booked before a house sale, a loft conversion, or new insulation being fitted, and during probate clearances where decades of stored belongings need sorting. We check boxes for photographs, documents and valuables as we go, so nothing important leaves the house unnoticed.

Old suitcases, Christmas decorations, cot beds, water tanks, offcut carpet — whatever is up there, it comes down, gets sorted, and is donated, recycled or disposed of properly.

What's included

  • Everything carried down and loaded — no lifting for you
  • Boxes checked for photos, documents and valuables

Loft Clearance — your questions answered

Is it safe to clear a loft that isn't boarded?

Yes — our team uses crawl boards to spread weight across the joists and works carefully around wiring and pipework. It is far safer than balancing on joists yourself, and it is something we do week in, week out.

Can you remove an old cold water tank?

Usually, yes. Redundant tanks can often be cut down and removed through the hatch. We will confirm when we see it — occasionally very large tanks pre-date the hatch and need a different approach, and we will tell you honestly if so.

Will you check boxes before disposing of them?

Yes, as standard. Lofts are where photographs, paperwork and keepsakes end up, so we open and check boxes as we clear, setting aside anything that looks personal or valuable for you to review.

How long does a loft clearance take?

Most domestic lofts are cleared in one to three hours depending on how full they are and how easy the access is. We will give you a time estimate with your quote.

Can you clear the loft as part of a full house clearance?

Of course — lofts are included in our full house clearance service whenever you ask. Booking them together is cheaper than two separate visits.
