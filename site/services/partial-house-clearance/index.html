At a glance

HouseClear provides partial house clearance across Cheshire, Flintshire, Denbighshire, Liverpool, Wrexham and the Wirral, with fixed quotes usually provided the same day.

  • Registered waste carrier — digital waste transfer note with every job
  • Fully insured, uniformed local team
  • Fixed quotes from photos, a description or a free visit
  • Reusable items donated, resold or recycled before anything is disposed of
  • VAT invoices provided; finance available on larger clearances

Not every clearance is a whole house. Perhaps you are downsizing and only need the spare room and garage emptied, decluttering before a house sale, or removing old furniture ahead of a renovation. Our partial clearance service removes only the items you choose.

You show us (or send photos of) what needs to go, we give you a fixed price for those items, and we remove them carefully — protecting floors and doorways as we work. Everything else in the home stays exactly where it is.

It is a flexible, affordable alternative to hiring a skip: no permits, no loading it yourself, and no skip sitting on the drive for a week. We load, sweep up and leave the same day.

Partial House Clearance — your questions answered

Is there a minimum job size?

Our minimum charge covers roughly a single-item collection — an old sofa or a washing machine, for example. From there, pricing is based on volume, so you only pay for the space your items take in the van.

How does pricing work for a part clearance?

Send us photos or a list of what needs to go and we will quote a fixed price. Because we charge by volume rather than by time, the price you are quoted is the price you pay.

Can you take items from upstairs or awkward spaces?

Yes — that is a normal day for us. We dismantle beds and wardrobes where needed, protect the staircase and walls, and have the equipment to handle heavy or awkward pieces safely.

Is a part clearance cheaper than a skip?

Usually, yes. A typical part load costs less than skip hire once you factor in permits and your own time loading it — and unlike a skip, we do the lifting, we come when booked, and reusable items are donated rather than landfilled.

Can you separate items for charity?

Gladly. Tell us which items you would like donated and we will keep them separate from waste. Good furniture in clean condition is usually rehomed through local charities.
