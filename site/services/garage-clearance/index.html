At a glance

HouseClear provides garage clearance across Cheshire, Flintshire, Denbighshire, Liverpool, Wrexham and the Wirral, with fixed quotes usually provided the same day.

  • Registered waste carrier — digital waste transfer note with every job
  • Fully insured, uniformed local team

Garages have a way of filling themselves. Old tools, half-used paint, offcuts of timber, broken bikes, boxes that never got unpacked from the last move — until one day the car does not fit any more. Our garage clearance service deals with all of it in a single visit.

We clear attached garages, detached garages and blocks, sorting as we load: metals and timber for recycling, usable tools and bikes for rehoming, and genuine junk for licensed disposal. Paints and certain chemicals need special handling, and we will advise on those honestly when we quote.

Garage clearances are often booked alongside a house or garden clearance, but plenty of customers book them alone — usually before a house move or simply to reclaim the space.

What's included

  • Attached and detached garages, carports and storage blocks
  • Tools, timber, metal, bikes and bulky junk removed
  • Metals and timber separated for recycling
  • Usable tools and equipment rehomed where possible
  • Advice on paint, chemicals and other special-handling items
  • Floor swept and space left genuinely usable

Garage Clearance — your questions answered

How long does a garage clearance take?

Most single garages are cleared in one to two hours. Doubles and heavily loaded garages take a little longer, but almost all are finished the same morning or afternoon we arrive.

Can you take paint, oil and chemicals?

Part-used paint and household chemicals need licensed hazardous handling, which we can arrange or advise on — some items are best taken to your local household waste recycling centre free of charge, and we will tell you honestly which is cheaper for you.

Do you take metal and scrap?

Yes, and it works in your favour — scrap metal has value, and we reflect that in your quote. Timber and cardboard are also separated for recycling rather than thrown in with general waste.

What about heavy items like workbenches and machinery?

No problem. We bring the manpower and equipment for heavy lifting, and we can dismantle benches, shelving and racking as part of the job.

Can you clear the garage while I'm at work?

Yes, as long as we can access the garage safely. Many customers leave the garage unlocked or leave a key with a neighbour, and we send photos of the cleared space when we are done.
