At a glance

HouseClear provides flat clearance across Cheshire, Flintshire, Denbighshire, Liverpool, Wrexham and the Wirral, with fixed quotes usually provided the same day.

  • Registered waste carrier — digital waste transfer note with every job
  • Fully insured, uniformed local team

Clearing a flat brings challenges a house does not: stairs or small lifts, communal hallways that must be protected and left spotless, managing agents with rules about timings, and city parking that needs planning. HouseClear handles flat and apartment clearances every week, from Liverpool city centre blocks to converted terraces in Chester.

We plan access before we arrive — checking parking, lift dimensions and any building requirements with you or the managing agent — so the clearance runs smoothly and neighbours are barely aware it is happening.

Whether it is an end of tenancy, a probate clearance, or a landlord turning a flat around between tenants, we clear the property completely, protect communal areas as we work, and leave both the flat and the hallway clean behind us.

What's included

  • Upper-floor flats, maisonettes and apartments — with or without lifts
  • Communal hallways protected and left clean
  • Liaison with managing agents, concierges and building managers
  • City-centre parking and loading restrictions planned in advance
  • Furniture dismantled where staircases demand it
  • Full compliance paperwork for landlords and agents

Flat Clearance — your questions answered

Do you charge extra for stairs?

Access is factored into your quote up front, so there are no surprises on the day. A third-floor flat with no lift takes longer than a ground-floor one, and we simply price that honestly from the start.

Can you deal with the building's managing agent?

Yes. If the block requires booking a service lift, providing insurance details or working set hours, we will handle that directly with the agent or concierge — just put us in touch.

What about parking in city centres?

We plan it before we arrive. Where loading bays or permits are needed — common in central Liverpool and Chester — we arrange them, and any unavoidable costs are shown clearly in your quote rather than added later.

Can large furniture come out of a small flat?

Almost always. Sofas, beds and wardrobes that went in can come out, and where they genuinely cannot, we dismantle them. It is a routine part of flat clearances for our team.

Do you clear flats for landlords between tenancies?

Frequently. We clear abandoned belongings, remove old furniture, and provide the waste transfer documentation landlords need for their records — often within 48 hours so relet works can start.
