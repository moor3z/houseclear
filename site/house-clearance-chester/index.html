At a glance

HouseClear provides full and partial house clearances throughout Chester, including Hoole, Handbridge, Upton, Blacon, with quotes usually provided the same day.

  • Local team covering the whole of Chester
  • Registered waste carrier — digital waste transfer note with every clearance
  • Fully insured; probate, bereavement, landlord and hoarder clearances handled
  • Reusable furniture donated to local charities where possible
  • Fixed quotes from photos or a free, no-obligation visit

From Victorian terraces in Hoole and Handbridge to apartments inside the city walls and family homes in Upton, Vicars Cross and Blacon, HouseClear clears properties across every part of Chester — quickly, respectfully and with full compliance paperwork.

Chester's older housing brings its own challenges: narrow terraced streets with on-street parking, steep staircases, and city-centre flats above the Rows where access needs planning. We handle these details before we arrive, so the day itself runs smoothly and your neighbours barely notice.

Much of our Chester work comes through estate agents, solicitors and word of mouth — probate and bereavement clearances, landlord turnarounds around the university, and downsizing moves. Good furniture is donated to charities in and around the city, and everything else is recycled or disposed of at licensed facilities with a digital waste transfer note provided.

As a Roman city with a large stock of period and listed housing, Chester sees more probate and estate house clearances than most towns its size, and much of our work here comes through local solicitors, estate agents and word of mouth. We are used to working to a completion date set by a Chester conveyancer, coordinating keys through agents, and providing VAT invoices addressed to an estate — every clearance priced individually and carried out by our own insured, uniformed team.

House clearance in Chester — common questions

Can you clear terraced houses with no driveway?

Yes — a large part of Chester is exactly that. We plan parking in advance, work quickly and considerately on narrow streets like those in Hoole and Handbridge, and protect shared alleyways and frontages as we load.

Do you clear student and rental properties in Chester?

Frequently. Landlords and agents across the city use us for end of tenancy clearances, particularly over the summer changeover. We can quote from photos and collect keys from your agent's office.

Can you handle a probate clearance while we're not in Chester?

Yes. Many executors we work for live outside the area. We collect keys from a solicitor or agent, send photos before and after, and manage the whole clearance remotely.

How soon can you provide a quote in Chester?

Usually the same day. Send photos over WhatsApp, fill in the quote form, or book a free visit — we are in and around Chester daily, so visits are easy to arrange.

Do you clear listed and period properties in Chester?

Yes — a large share of Chester's housing is period or listed, and we clear these carefully, protecting original features, staircases and flooring as we work. For city-centre and conservation-area properties we plan parking and loading in advance so the clearance causes no disruption.

How much do house clearances in Chester cost?

Chester house clearances are priced by volume — how much the contents fill in the van — adjusted for access and any items needing special handling. Send photos for a fixed same-day quote; where furniture or contents have resale value, we offset that against the price. There are no hidden charges and the quote you agree is the price you pay.
