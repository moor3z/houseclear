Frequently asked questions

How do I get a house clearance quote?

Send a few photos over WhatsApp, use our quote form, or book a free visit. We price by volume and confirm a fixed quote — usually the same day — with no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. HouseClear is a registered waste carrier with full public liability insurance, and every clearance comes with a digital waste transfer note and a VAT invoice.

What happens to the items you clear?

Reusable furniture and household goods are donated to local charities or resold, recyclables are recovered at licensed facilities, and only genuine waste goes for disposal.

Which areas do you cover?

Cheshire, Flintshire, Denbighshire, Liverpool, Wrexham and the whole Wirral peninsula — including Chester, Ellesmere Port, Northwich, Mold, Rhyl and Prestatyn.

Can you help with probate or bereavement clearances?

Yes — sensitively and at your pace. We set aside keepsakes and documents as we work, liaise with solicitors where needed, and provide paperwork suitable for estate accounting.

Do you offer finance on larger clearances?

On larger jobs, yes — spread-cost options are available where relevant. Ask when you enquire and we will explain what applies to your clearance.
